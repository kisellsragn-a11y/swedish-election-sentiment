**DEFENCE / STATEMENT OF POSITION**

I dispute the claimant's claim for SEK 12,000, together with interest
and costs, and request that the claim be dismissed to the extent that
the claimant cannot prove the amounts and legal basis relied upon.

I specifically dispute the alleged SEK 3,500 charge for non-returned
keys and the SEK 1,500 claim for cleaning and minor repairs. I also
dispute the claimant's reliance on a key-return document bearing a
signature which I did not make.

**1. Contract A and Contract B --- change in the contractual and payment
arrangement**

There were two contractual arrangements relevant to the tenancy. Under
Contract A, the arrangement was with Younex AB and there was an
established business process for invoicing rent and making payment. Rent
was invoiced in the ordinary course, and there was an established method
by which I was expected to pay.

Subsequently, Contract B was entered into in a different contractual
arrangement concerning the accommodation. Contract B did not contain the
same established invoicing and payment mechanism as Contract A. After
Contract B was signed, I was not provided with an invoice or clear
payment instructions for the rent in the same manner as under Contract
A.

The change in contractual arrangements occurred in the context of an
ownership issue involving the claimant and his brother and the threat
that I could be required to leave the property. I entered into Contract
B in that context and in an effort to regularise the tenancy.

I do not contend that the existence of Contract B by itself extinguished
any rent obligation. Rather, I ask the Court to consider the parties'
established payment practice, the change from Contract A to Contract B,
the absence of an invoicing/payment mechanism under Contract B, and the
claimant's conduct after Contract B was signed when determining the
parties' respective responsibilities and any interest or consequential
costs.

In particular, the claimant should explain why, after changing the
contractual arrangement and failing to provide an invoice or clear
payment method, he proceeded to enforcement without first providing a
clear demand and reasonable opportunity to pay.

**2. The SEK 3,500 deposit**

The rental agreement states that a SEK 3,500 deposit was paid and was
refundable within five working days after leaving. The claimant's
submission to Kronofogden also states that the SEK 3,500 deposit was set
off ("Deposition 3 500 kr kvittad").

The claimant must therefore explain precisely what amount was retained
from the deposit, on what contractual and factual basis, and how that
amount is treated in the calculation of the present claim. The claimant
should not receive double recovery for the same alleged loss.

**3. Alleged non-return of keys --- signature expressly disputed**

The rental documents show that two keys were initially provided: one key
to the tenant's room and one key to the entrance. I do not dispute that
the contract contains a provision concerning a SEK 3,500 charge for lost
keys. I dispute, however, that I failed to return the keys.

The claimant relies on a document entitled "Key receipt and return". The
receipt section records the initial receipt of the keys. However, the
document contains a signature attributed to me which I did not make. I
did not sign the document or authorize anyone to sign it on my behalf.

I therefore expressly dispute the authenticity and evidential value of
that signature. I ask the Court not to treat the document as an
admission by me that the keys were not returned unless the claimant
establishes that I actually signed it.

If the claimant relies on that signature, I request that the claimant be
required to identify when, where and by whom the document was signed and
to provide the best available evidence concerning its authenticity and
the circumstances in which it was created.

I do not ask the Court to make a finding of criminal wrongdoing in this
civil proceeding. My position is that I did not sign the document and
that its authenticity is disputed.

**4. Cleaning and minor repairs --- SEK 1,500**

The claimant's Kronofogden material states, in substance, that cleaning
and minor repair of the room were carried out by the claimant personally
on 1 October 2025 and that an expense of SEK 1,500 was claimed.

I dispute that this amount has been sufficiently established as a
recoverable loss. The claimant should prove what work was necessary, why
I was legally responsible for it, how the SEK 1,500 was calculated, and
what evidence shows that the claimed work and amount were actually
incurred.

**5. The Saberg Bygg AB invoice requires clarification**

A separate invoice from Saberg Bygg AB has been presented. It totals SEK
5,000 including VAT and includes, among other items, SEK 3,500 for the
purchase and replacement of two locks, as well as charges relating to
cleaning, repair of holes, and removal/transport of a carpet and sofa.

This evidence must be reconciled with the claimant's statement to
Kronofogden that the cleaning and minor repair were carried out
personally. The claimant should explain whether the work was performed
personally, by Saberg Bygg AB, or partly by each, and which specific
invoice items are being claimed from me.

In particular, the claimant should explain why a SEK 3,500
lock-replacement item is claimed in circumstances where the claimant
also relies on a contractual SEK 3,500 lost-key charge and has already
stated that the SEK 3,500 deposit was set off. I object to any double
recovery for the same alleged key-related loss.

**6. Rent claim and calculation**

The claimant also seeks rent for July--September 2025 on the basis of a
three-calendar-month notice period. I dispute the calculation to the
extent that it does not properly take into account the date notice was
given, the contractual terms, payments already made, and any credits or
set-offs.

The claimant should provide a complete calculation showing the rent
claimed for each month, the due date, all payments received, all
deductions or set-offs, and the precise amount said to remain
outstanding.

**7. Interest**

Because the underlying principal claim is disputed, I also dispute
interest to the extent it is calculated on amounts that are not owed.

In assessing interest, I ask the Court to consider the transition from
Contract A to Contract B, the established invoicing and payment practice
under Contract A, the absence of the same clear invoicing or payment
mechanism after Contract B was signed, and whether the claimant gave a
sufficiently clear demand and reasonable opportunity to pay before
escalating the matter.

I do not argue that the absence of an invoice automatically extinguished
a contractual rent obligation. Rather, these circumstances are relevant
to whether the claimant has established the correct due date, default,
and basis for the interest claimed.

**8. Legal, Kronofogden and court costs**

The claimant seeks reimbursement for costs including approximately SEK
380 for the Kronofogden application, SEK 2,000 for legal advice, and SEK
600 for the court application. I dispute these costs to the extent that
they are not recoverable under the applicable rules, have not been
sufficiently established, or arise from amounts that are themselves not
owed.

The claimant should identify the legal basis for each cost and provide
evidence of the amount actually incurred and, where relevant, paid.

I also ask the Court to consider the circumstances in which these costs
arose. Under Contract A there had been an established process for
invoicing and payment. After Contract B was signed, the claimant did not
provide the same established invoicing or clear payment mechanism.
Instead, the matter was escalated to Kronofogden without first giving me
a clear demand and payment instructions under the new arrangement.

I therefore ask the Court to distinguish between the underlying
contractual obligation, interest on any amount actually due, and costs
arising from the claimant's decision to escalate the matter to
Kronofogden and court. The mere fact that the claimant incurred a cost
does not by itself establish that I am legally liable for that cost.

**9. The Court's determinations requested**

I respectfully ask the Court to determine, in particular:

1.  which contractual arrangement governs the relevant rent and payment
    obligations;

2.  how the change from Contract A to Contract B affected the
    established invoicing and payment procedure;

3.  whether the claimant gave adequate notice and a reasonable
    opportunity to pay before commencing enforcement;

4.  whether the claimant has established that the keys were not
    returned;

5.  whether the disputed signature has sufficient evidentiary value to
    establish receipt or non-return of the keys;

6.  whether the SEK 3,500 deposit has already been applied against any
    of the claimed losses;

7.  whether the SEK 1,500 cleaning/repair claim is supported by evidence
    and is not duplicated by the later contractor invoice;

8.  whether the SEK 3,500 lock-replacement item is being claimed in
    addition to, or instead of, the contractual SEK 3,500 lost-key
    charge; and

9.  whether the claimed interest and legal/enforcement costs are legally
    recoverable in the circumstances.

**10. Evidence and documents**

I rely on the documents already submitted or disclosed in this matter,
including:

-   Contract A and the established invoicing/payment records under that
    arrangement;

-   Contract B and the relevant provisions concerning rent, deposit,
    keys and payment;

-   the key receipt and return document, including the disputed
    signature attributed to me;

-   the claimant's Kronofogden submission describing the rent, key
    charge, deposit set-off, and cleaning/repair claim;

-   the Malmö District Court material referring to the SEK 1,500
    cleaning and repair amount; and

-   the Saberg Bygg AB invoice for cleaning, repairs, removal/transport
    and lock replacement.

I reserve the right to submit further evidence and to respond to any
additional documents or explanations produced by the claimant.

**11. Summary and request**

The central issues are whether the claimant has proved the alleged
non-return of the keys, whether the disputed signature can properly be
attributed to me, whether the cleaning and repair claim is established
and recoverable, how the SEK 3,500 deposit has been accounted for,
whether the Saberg Bygg AB invoice duplicates any claimed loss, and
whether the claimant's conduct following the change from Contract A to
Contract B affects the claimed interest and costs.

I respectfully request that the claimant's claim be dismissed to the
extent that it is not proved, and that the Court award costs in
accordance with the applicable rules.
